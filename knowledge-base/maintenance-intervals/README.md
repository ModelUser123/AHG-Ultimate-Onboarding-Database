## Maintenance Schedule Overview

### Normal vs. Severe Service

**Normal Service**: Most daily driving that includes a mix of highway and city driving.

**Severe Service** (any of the following):
- Frequent short trips (less than 5 miles)
- Driving in dusty conditions
- Towing or hauling heavy loads
- Extended idling
- Extreme hot or cold temperatures
- Stop-and-go traffic
- Off-road driving

**When in doubt, use severe service intervals.**

## Engine Maintenance

### Oil Changes

| Oil Type | Normal Interval | Severe Interval |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Conventional | 5,000-7,500 miles | 3,000-5,000 miles |
| Synthetic blend | 7,500 miles | 5,000 miles |
| Full synthetic | 10,000-15,000 miles | 7,500-10,000 miles |
| Diesel | 5,000-7,500 miles | 5,000 miles |

**Time Interval**: Change at least annually, regardless of mileage.

### Spark Plugs

| Plug Type | Interval |
|-----------|----------|
| Copper | 20,000-30,000 miles |
| Platinum | 60,000-100,000 miles |
| Iridium | 100,000-120,000 miles |
| Double platinum | 100,000 miles |

### Ignition Components

| Component | Inspect | Replace |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Spark plug wires | 30,000 miles | 60,000-100,000 miles |
| Distributor cap/rotor | 30,000 miles | 50,000-100,000 miles |
| Ignition coils | When problem occurs | As needed |

### Timing Belt/Chain

| Component | Interval |
|-----------|----------|
| Timing belt | 60,000-100,000 miles |
| Timing chain | Inspect at 100,000+ miles |
| Water pump | Replace with timing belt |
| Tensioners/idlers | Replace with timing belt |

**Critical**: Timing belt failure on interference engine causes major engine damage.

### Drive Belts

| Component | Inspect | Replace |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Serpentine belt | 30,000 miles | 60,000-100,000 miles |
| V-belts | 15,000 miles | 30,000-50,000 miles |
| Belt tensioner | With belt | 100,000 miles |

### PCV Valve

| Interval | Action |
|----------|--------|
| 30,000 miles | Inspect |
| 60,000 miles | Replace |

## Transmission Maintenance

### Automatic Transmission

| Service | Normal Interval | Severe Interval |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Fluid check | Every oil change | Every oil change |
| Fluid & filter change | 60,000 miles | 30,000 miles |
| Flush | 60,000-100,000 miles | 30,000-60,000 miles |

### Manual Transmission

| Service | Interval |
|---------|----------|
| Fluid check | 30,000 miles |
| Fluid change | 30,000-60,000 miles |

### CVT Transmission

| Service | Interval |
|---------|----------|
| Fluid change | 30,000-60,000 miles |
| Severe service | 25,000-30,000 miles |

### Transfer Case (4WD/AWD)

| Service | Interval |
|---------|----------|
| Fluid check | 30,000 miles |
| Fluid change | 30,000 miles |

### Differential

| Service | Normal Interval | Severe Interval |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Fluid check | 30,000 miles | 15,000 miles |
| Fluid change | 30,000-60,000 miles | 15,000-30,000 miles |

## Brake Maintenance

| Component | Inspect Interval |
|-----------|------------------|
| Brake pads/shoes | Every oil change |
| Rotors/drums | Every oil change |
| Brake lines | Annual |
| Brake fluid | Every oil change |
| Parking brake | Annual |

### Brake Fluid

| Service | Interval |
|---------|----------|
| Flush/replace | Every 2-3 years |
| Check condition | Every oil change |
| Check level | Every oil change |

| Component | Expected Life |

| Brake pads (rear) | 40,000-80,000 miles |

| Brake hoses | 100,000 miles/10 years |
| Brake calipers | 100,000+ miles |

## Cooling System

### Coolant Service

| Coolant Type | Interval |
|--------------|----------|
| Traditional (green) | 2 years/30,000 miles |
| Extended life (OAT/HOAT) | 5 years/100,000-150,000 miles |
| After first drain | Follow extended life interval |

### Cooling System Components

| Component | Inspect | Replace |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Hoses | Every oil change | Every 4-5 years |
| Thermostat | When problem occurs | 100,000 miles |
| Radiator cap | Annual | 5 years |
| Water pump | With timing belt | 100,000 miles |
| Radiator | When leaking | As needed |

## Tires & Wheels

### Tire Maintenance

| Service | Interval |
|---------|----------|
| Check pressure | Monthly |
| Rotation | 5,000-7,500 miles |
| Balance | With rotation or when vibration |
| Alignment check | Annual or 12,000 miles |
| Inspection | Every oil change |

### Tire Life Expectations

| Tire Type | Expected Life |
|-----------|---------------|
| All-season | 50,000-70,000 miles |
| Performance | 30,000-50,000 miles |
| All-terrain | 40,000-60,000 miles |
| Winter | 40,000 miles (4 seasons) |

### Tire Age

- Replace tires older than 6-10 years regardless of tread
- Check DOT date code on sidewall

## Severe Service Conditions

### Vehicles Requiring Severe Schedule

- Taxi/rideshare
- Delivery vehicles
- Towing regularly
- Off-road use
- Short trips only
- Dusty environments
- Extreme temperatures

### Severe Service Adjustments

| Service | Normal | Severe |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Oil change | 7,500 miles | 3,000-5,000 miles |
| Air filter | 30,000 miles | 15,000 miles |
| Transmission | 60,000 miles | 30,000 miles |
| Coolant | 5 years | 3 years |
| Brake inspection | 15,000 miles | 7,500 miles |

## Maintenance Inspection Checklist

### Every Oil Change

- [ ] Engine oil and filter
- [ ] Check all fluid levels
- [ ] Inspect brake pads/rotors
- [ ] Check tire pressure and condition
- [ ] Inspect belts and hoses
- [ ] Check lights
- [ ] Visual inspection under vehicle
- [ ] Reset maintenance reminder

### Every 15,000 Miles

- [ ] All items above
- [ ] Rotate tires
- [ ] Inspect air filter
- [ ] Inspect cabin air filter
- [ ] Inspect battery
- [ ] Inspect suspension components
- [ ] Check exhaust system

### Every 30,000 Miles

- [ ] All items above
- [ ] Replace air filter
- [ ] Replace cabin air filter
- [ ] Inspect fuel system
- [ ] Inspect cooling system
- [ ] Check power steering fluid
- [ ] Inspect CV boots and axles
- [ ] Check wheel bearings

### Every 60,000 Miles

- [ ] All items above
- [ ] Transmission service
- [ ] Brake fluid flush
- [ ] Inspect timing belt/chain
- [ ] Replace spark plugs (if due)
- [ ] Differential service
- [ ] Replace PCV valve

### Every 100,000 Miles

- [ ] All items above
- [ ] Replace timing belt (if equipped)
- [ ] Replace water pump
- [ ] Replace spark plugs
- [ ] Major cooling system service
- [ ] Replace serpentine belt
- [ ] Replace tensioners and idlers
