## Inspection Overview

### Inspection Types

| Type | Coverage Area | Requirements |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| Safety Only | Non-attainment exempt counties | Safety items |
| Safety + Emissions | Designated counties | Safety + OBD-II |
| Two-Year | New vehicles | First 2 years exempt |

### Emissions-Required Counties

**Currently Required** (subject to change):
- Brazoria, Collin, Dallas, Denton, El Paso
- Ellis, Fort Bend, Galveston, Harris, Johnson
- Kaufman, Montgomery, Parker, Rockwall, Tarrant
- Travis, Williamson

### Inspection Valid Period

- **Standard Inspection**: 1 year from issue date
- **New Vehicles**: First inspection due 2 years from original registration

## Inspector Requirements

### Becoming Certified

**Requirements**:
1. Complete DPS-approved training course
2. Pass written examination
3. Pass practical examination
4. Background check
5. Work at licensed inspection station

### Inspector Responsibilities

- Conduct thorough inspections
- Follow DPS procedures exactly
- Document all inspections
- Report rejected vehicles
- Maintain certification

### Continuing Education

- Annual refresher training required
- Stay current on rule changes
- Maintain valid certification

## Safety Inspection Items

### Horn

**Pass Requirements**:
- Horn must work
- Must be audible at 200 feet
- Original equipment or equivalent

**Rejection Criteria**:
- No horn function
- Inaudible
- Air horns (unless OE)

### Windshield Wipers

**Pass Requirements**:
- Must clear windshield effectively
- Blades in serviceable condition
- All speeds function (if multi-speed)

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Non-functional wipers
- Severely worn blades (streaking/missing sections)
- Missing wiper arms

### Mirror(s)

**Pass Requirements**:
- Inside rearview mirror required
- Left outside mirror required
- All mirrors properly mounted and functional
- No excessive cracks or damage

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Missing required mirrors
- Loose mounting
- Severe damage impairing visibility

### Steering

**Pass Requirements**:
- No excessive play
- All steering components secure
- No binding or sticking
- Power steering (if equipped) functioning

**Steering Play Limits**:
| Wheel Diameter | Maximum Play |
|----------------|--------------|
| 16" | 2" |
| 18" | 2-1/4" |
| 20" | 2-1/2" |
| 22" | 2-3/4" |

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Excessive play
- Loose steering components
- Binding steering
- Non-functional power steering

### Brakes

**Pass Requirements**:
- Service brakes functional on all wheels
- Parking brake holds vehicle
- Brake pedal reserve
- No leaks
- Brake lines in good condition
- Warning light not illuminated

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Brakes inoperative on any wheel
- Metal-to-metal contact
- Leaking brake lines or cylinders
- Parking brake doesn't hold
- Brake warning light on
- Inadequate pedal reserve (less than 1/3 travel)

### Tires/Wheels

**Pass Requirements**:
- Minimum 2/32" tread depth
- No exposed cords or fabric
- Properly inflated
- All lug nuts present and tight
- No cracks in wheels
- Matching tires on same axle

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Tread depth below 2/32"
- Exposed cords/fabric
- Bulges or separations
- Missing lug nuts
- Cracked wheels
- Mismatched tire sizes on same axle

### Wheel Bearings

**Pass Requirements**:
- No excessive play
- No noise indicating wear
- Properly lubricated

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Excessive play
- Grinding or growling noise
- Evidence of failure

### Ball Joints, Tie Rods, Steering Components

**Pass Requirements**:
- No excessive play
- All components secure
- Boots intact (if equipped)

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Measurable play in load-carrying ball joints
- Loose or worn tie rod ends
- Missing or severely damaged boots

### Suspension

**Pass Requirements**:
- Springs intact
- Shocks/struts functional
- No broken components
- Properly aligned

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Broken springs
- Severely leaking shocks
- Broken suspension components
- Excessive sag affecting ride height

### Headlamps

**Pass Requirements**:
- Both high and low beam functional
- Proper aim
- Correct color (white or yellow)
- Approved equipment

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Non-functional bulb(s)
- Improper aim (too high/low)
- Improper color
- Cracked or broken lenses (if moisture intrusion)
- Aftermarket HID/LED not DOT approved

### Tail Lamps

**Pass Requirements**:
- Both tail lamps functional
- Red in color
- Visible from 1000 feet

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Non-functional
- Improper color
- Broken lens (if white light visible)

### Stop Lamps

**Pass Requirements**:
- All stop lamps functional
- Red in color
- Illuminated when brake applied
- Third brake light (if equipped from factory)

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Non-functional
- Improper color
- CHMSL (third light) non-functional

### License Plate Light

**Pass Requirements**:
- Illuminates rear plate
- White light only
- Functional

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Non-functional
- Improper color

### Turn Signals/Hazards

**Pass Requirements**:
- All signals functional
- Proper flash rate (1-2 per second)
- Proper color (amber front, red or amber rear)
- Hazards flash all signals simultaneously

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Non-functional
- Flash too fast or slow
- Improper color

### Headlamp Aim

**Procedure**:
1. Vehicle on level surface
2. Tires properly inflated
3. No load in vehicle
4. Check at 25 feet
5. Use approved aiming equipment or wall chart

**Specifications**:
- High-intensity zone should be slightly below center
- Horizontal aim within ±4 inches of straight ahead

### Beam Indicator

**Pass Requirements**:
- High beam indicator on dash functional
- Illuminates when high beams on

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Non-functional indicator

### Exhaust System

**Pass Requirements**:
- Complete exhaust from engine to tailpipe
- No leaks
- Properly supported
- Tailpipe exit behind passenger compartment

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Leaking exhaust
- Missing components
- Tailpipe exiting beneath passenger area

### Exhaust Emission System

**Pass Requirements**:
- All emission components present
- Not tampered
- Catalyst present (if originally equipped)

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Missing catalytic converter
- Removed or bypassed emission controls
- Non-factory emission modifications

### Gas Caps

**Pass Requirements**:
- Cap present
- Seals properly
- Correct type for vehicle

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Missing cap
- Damaged seal
- Wrong type (not sealing)

### Window Tint

**Texas Requirements**:
- Front windshield: Only top 5" or AS-1 line
- Front side windows: 25% VLT minimum
- Rear side windows: Any tint permitted
- Rear window: Any tint permitted

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Tint on front windshield below allowed area
- Front side windows below 25% VLT

### Windshield

**Pass Requirements**:
- No cracks in driver's direct view
- Not obstructing vision
- No objects hanging that obstruct view

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Cracks directly in driver's line of sight
- Multiple cracks obstructing vision
- Objects blocking view

### Safety Seat Belts

**Pass Requirements**:
- All belts present (as originally equipped)
- Functional latch
- No fraying or damage

**Rejection Criteria**:
- Missing belts
- Non-functional latches
- Cut or frayed belts

## Emissions Inspection

### OBD-II Inspection

**Requirements** (1996 and newer gasoline vehicles):
- Connect scan tool to DLC
- Retrieve readiness monitor status
- Check for stored DTCs
- Check MIL (Check Engine Light) status

**Pass Requirements**:
- MIL not illuminated (engine running)
- No emissions-related DTCs
- Required monitors complete

**Readiness Monitor Requirements**:
| Model Year | Allowed Incomplete |
|------------|-------------------|
| 1996-2000 | 2 monitors |
| 2001+ | 1 monitor |

**Required Monitors** (if applicable):
- Catalyst
- Heated Catalyst
- Evaporative System
- Secondary Air
- A/C Refrigerant
- Oxygen Sensor
- Oxygen Sensor Heater
- EGR

### Diesel Emissions

**Testing**:
- Visual inspection of smoke
- Tampering check
- No scan test required

---

## Rejection Criteria

### Immediate Rejection

Vehicle fails if ANY of the following:
1. Safety item doesn't meet requirements
2. Emissions test failure
3. MIL illuminated (engine running)
4. Emission control tampering
5. OBD-II system failure

### Pass with Advisory

Some conditions may pass but warrant customer notification:
- Tire tread near minimum
- Brakes near minimum
- Wipers showing wear
- Minor equipment concerns

### Documentation of Rejection

When rejecting a vehicle:
1. Complete rejection sticker/documentation
2. Explain all rejection reasons to customer
3. Document in system
4. Provide written explanation

## Special Inspections

### Commercial Vehicles

**Additional Requirements**:
- DOT numbers if required
- Fire extinguisher (if required)
- Emergency equipment
- Mud flaps

### Trailers

**Requirements**:
- Lighting functional
- Safety chains
- Proper hitch
- Tires and wheels

### Motorcycles

**Requirements**:
- Handlebars (max height)
- Footrests
- Lighting
- Horn
- Mirrors
- Brakes

### Exempt Vehicles

**No Inspection Required**:
- Vehicles over 25 years old (as of 2022)
- New vehicles (first 2 years)
- Farm vehicles with farm plates
- Certain government vehicles

## Documentation Requirements

### For Each Inspection

**Record**:
- Date and time
- Vehicle information (VIN, make, model, year)
- Odometer reading
- Inspection results (pass/fail)
- Inspector ID
- Station number
- Fee collected

### Record Retention

- Maintain records for 2 years minimum
- Make available for DPS audit
- Secure storage of stickers/documents

### Sticker Application

**Procedure**:
1. Remove old sticker completely
2. Clean windshield area
3. Apply new sticker in correct location (left side, above line of sight)
4. Ensure sticker is flat and fully adhered

## Fees and Pricing

### State-Set Fees

| Inspection Type | Maximum Fee |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Safety Only | $7.00 |
| Safety + Emissions | $7.00 (inspection) |
| | + Emissions fee |
| Motorcycle | $7.00 |
| Trailer | $7.00 |
| Commercial | $7.00 + |

*Note: Fees are set by state and subject to change*

### Emissions Fees

**Additional fees collected for emissions testing**:
- $16.50-$28.50 depending on program type

### Re-Inspection

**Within 15 Days**:
- Free re-inspection for failed items only
- Must be at same station
- Must be same vehicle

**After 15 Days**:
- Full inspection required
- Full fee charged
