## Coolant Specifications

### Coolant Types

| Type | Color | Manufacturer |
|------|-------|--------------|
| IAT (Inorganic) | Green | Traditional |
| OAT (Organic Acid) | Orange, red, blue | GM Dexcool, VW/Audi |
| HOAT (Hybrid) | Yellow, turquoise, purple | Ford, Chrysler, Asian |
| P-HOAT | Pink, blue | Toyota, Asian |

### By Manufacturer

| Manufacturer | Type | Color | Interval |
|--------------|------|-------|----------|
| **GM** | Dex-Cool (OAT) | Orange | 5 years/150k |
| **Ford** | Motorcraft Yellow (HOAT) | Yellow | 6 years/150k |
| **Chrysler** | OAT | Purple | 5 years/100k |
| **Toyota** | Super Long Life (P-HOAT) | Pink | 100k/5 years |
| **Honda** | Type 2 (HOAT) | Blue | 5 years/100k |
| **Hyundai/Kia** | HOAT | Green or pink | 120k/10 years |
| **Nissan** | Blue (HOAT) | Blue | 100k/5 years |
| **VW/Audi** | G13 (OAT) | Purple | 5 years |
| **BMW** | BMW-specific | Blue | 4 years |
| **Mercedes** | MB 325.0 | Blue | 5 years |

### Mixing Guidelines

**NEVER mix different coolant types without flushing**

| Mixing | Result |
|--------|--------|
| IAT + OAT | Gel formation, clogging |
| OAT + HOAT | Reduced protection |
| Same type | OK if same spec |

## Brake Fluid

### DOT Ratings

| DOT Type | Dry Boiling | Wet Boiling | Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| DOT 3 | 401°F | 284°F | Standard vehicles |
| DOT 4 | 446°F | 311°F | European, performance |
| DOT 5 | 500°F | 356°F | Military, antique (silicone) |
| DOT 5.1 | 500°F | 356°F | High-performance, ABS safe |

### By Manufacturer

| Manufacturer | Specification |
|--------------|---------------|
| **Most domestic** | DOT 3 |
| **European (BMW, VW, Mercedes)** | DOT 4, DOT 4+ |
| **Performance vehicles** | DOT 4 or DOT 5.1 |
| **Hybrids** | DOT 3 or 4 (check spec) |

**Note**: DOT 5 (silicone) is NOT compatible with DOT 3/4/5.1 and is not suitable for vehicles with ABS.

## Oil Change Intervals

### General Guidelines

| Oil Type | Normal Service | Severe Service |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| Conventional | 5,000 miles | 3,000 miles |
| Synthetic blend | 7,500 miles | 5,000 miles |
| Full synthetic | 10,000-15,000 miles | 7,500 miles |

### Severe Service Conditions

- Frequent short trips (< 5 miles)
- Dusty conditions
- Towing or hauling
- Extreme temperatures
- Stop-and-go traffic
- Extended idling

## Fluid Color Guide

### Identifying Leaks

| Color | Consistency | Likely Fluid |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Green, orange, pink | Watery, sweet smell | Coolant |
| Red, pink | Oily, thin | ATF or power steering |
| Light brown | Oily | New motor oil |
| Dark brown/black | Oily, thick | Used motor oil |
| Clear | Watery | AC condensation (normal) |
| Amber | Oily | Brake fluid |
| Dark | Thick | Gear oil |

## Important Notes

1. **Always verify specifications** with vehicle service information
2. **Oil viscosity may vary** by climate and driving conditions
3. **Don't mix fluids** of different types/specifications
4. **Synthetic is generally safe** as a substitute for conventional
5. **Some fluids are proprietary** - use OEM or exact specification only
6. **Fluid degradation** - even if not driven, fluids should be changed by time interval
