## General Safety Rules

### Shop Rules Everyone Must Follow

1. **Know the emergency exits and procedures**
2. **Report all injuries immediately, no matter how minor**
3. **Keep work areas clean and organized**
4. **No horseplay or running in the shop**
5. **No working under influence of drugs or alcohol**
6. **Wear appropriate PPE at all times**
7. **Never take shortcuts with safety**
8. **Ask if you're unsure about a procedure**

### Daily Safety Checklist

- [ ] PPE in good condition
- [ ] Work area clean
- [ ] Tools inspected
- [ ] Lifting equipment inspected
- [ ] Emergency equipment accessible
- [ ] Fire extinguishers visible
- [ ] First aid kit stocked
- [ ] Eyewash station functional
- [ ] Spill kits available
- [ ] Ventilation working

## Personal Protective Equipment

### Required PPE by Task

| Task | Eye | Hand | Foot | Hearing | Respiratory |
|------|-----|------|------|---------|-------------|
| General work | Safety glasses | Work gloves | Steel toe | - | - |
| Grinding | Face shield | Leather gloves | Steel toe | Plugs/muffs | - |
| Welding | Welding helmet | Welding gloves | Steel toe | - | Fume mask |
| Chemical handling | Goggles | Chemical resistant | Steel toe | - | As rated |
| Battery work | Goggles | Acid resistant | Steel toe | - | - |
| Spray painting | Goggles | Nitrile | Steel toe | - | Respirator |
| Under vehicle | Safety glasses | As needed | Steel toe | - | - |
| Air tools | Safety glasses | As needed | Steel toe | Plugs/muffs | - |

### PPE Standards

**Eye Protection**:
- Must meet ANSI Z87.1
- Side shields required
- Face shield for high-risk tasks

**Hand Protection**:
- Match glove to hazard
- Replace when worn or damaged
- Remove when operating machinery

**Foot Protection**:
- Steel toe required
- Oil-resistant sole
- Ankle support recommended

**Hearing Protection**:
- Required above 85 dB
- Plugs: NRR 20-30 dB
- Muffs: NRR 25-35 dB

**Respiratory Protection**:
- Must be properly fitted
- Filters matched to hazard
- Medical clearance may be required

## Fire Safety

### Fire Prevention

**Common Fire Hazards**:
- Flammable liquids (gasoline, solvents)
- Oily rags
- Sparks from grinding/welding
- Electrical faults
- Overheated equipment
- Smoking

**Prevention Measures**:
- Store flammables in approved cabinets
- Dispose of oily rags in metal containers
- Keep hot work away from flammables
- Maintain electrical equipment
- No smoking except in designated areas
- Clean up spills immediately

### Fire Extinguisher Types

| Class | Fire Type | Extinguisher Type |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| A | Ordinary combustibles | Water, ABC |
| B | Flammable liquids | ABC, CO2 |
| C | Electrical | ABC, CO2 |
| D | Combustible metals | Class D |

### PASS Method

**P** - Pull the pin
**A** - Aim at base of fire
**S** - Squeeze the handle
**S** - Sweep side to side

### Fire Extinguisher Maintenance

- Inspect monthly
- Service annually
- Hydrotest every 5-12 years
- Replace after use
- Keep visible and accessible

### Evacuation Procedures

1. Alert others
2. Call 911
3. Use nearest exit
4. Meet at designated assembly point
5. Account for all personnel
6. Do not re-enter until cleared

## Chemical Safety

### Safety Data Sheets (SDS)

**Required Information**:
1. Identification
2. Hazard identification
3. Composition/ingredients
4. First-aid measures
5. Fire-fighting measures
6. Accidental release measures
7. Handling and storage
8. Exposure controls/PPE
9. Physical/chemical properties
10. Stability and reactivity
11. Toxicological information
12. Ecological information
13. Disposal considerations
14. Transport information
15. Regulatory information
16. Other information

**SDS Requirements**:
- Must be accessible to all employees
- Available for every hazardous chemical
- Updated when new information available

### Common Automotive Chemicals

| Chemical | Hazards | PPE |
|----------|---------|-----|
| Gasoline | Flammable, vapor | Goggles, gloves, no sparks |
| Brake cleaner | Flammable, toxic | Goggles, gloves, ventilation |
| Coolant | Toxic if ingested | Gloves |
| Brake fluid | Corrosive | Goggles, gloves |
| Battery acid | Corrosive | Goggles, face shield, gloves |
| R-134a/R-1234yf | Frostbite, asphyxiant | Goggles, gloves |
| Used oil | Carcinogen with prolonged contact | Gloves |
| Parts cleaner | Flammable, toxic | Goggles, gloves, ventilation |

### Chemical Storage

**Requirements**:
- Flammable cabinet for flammables
- Separate incompatible chemicals
- Original or approved containers
- Proper labeling
- Secondary containment
- Away from heat sources

### Spill Response

1. **Alert**: Warn others of spill
2. **Contain**: Stop spread with absorbent
3. **Ventilate**: Open doors, turn on fans
4. **Absorb**: Use appropriate absorbent
5. **Clean**: Dispose of properly
6. **Report**: Document incident

## Lifting and Hoisting

### Vehicle Lifts

**Pre-Lift Inspection**:
- [ ] Lift arms/pads secure
- [ ] No hydraulic leaks
- [ ] Safety locks functional
- [ ] Clear around and under lift
- [ ] Weight capacity not exceeded

**Lift Procedure**:
1. Position vehicle properly
2. Set lift points at manufacturer locations
3. Raise slowly, checking stability
4. Engage safety locks
5. Test stability before working underneath

**Lift Points**:
- Use manufacturer-recommended points
- Avoid fuel tanks, floor pans, suspension
- Use adapters as needed
- Distribute weight evenly

**Lift Safety Rules**:
- Never exceed weight capacity
- Never rock vehicle on lift
- Always use safety locks
- Keep lift clean and maintained
- Annual inspection required

### Floor Jacks and Jack Stands

**Jack Safety**:
- Use on flat, solid surface
- Position at proper lift points
- Never rely on jack alone
- Always use jack stands

**Jack Stand Use**:
- Match capacity to vehicle weight
- Position at solid frame points
- Lower vehicle onto stands slowly
- Verify stability before working

### Engine Hoists/Cranes

**Before Use**:
- Inspect for damage
- Check capacity rating
- Verify chains/hooks condition
- Position on stable surface

**During Use**:
- Stay out of swing path
- Lift slowly and smoothly
- Keep load low when moving
- Never leave suspended loads unattended

## Tool Safety

### Hand Tools

**Requirements**:
- Use correct tool for job
- Keep tools in good condition
- Replace worn or damaged tools
- Store properly when not in use
- Carry tools properly (not in pockets)

### Power Tools

**General Safety**:
- Inspect before each use
- Use guards and shields
- Secure workpiece
- Disconnect when changing accessories
- Use proper eye and hearing protection

### Air Tools

**Specific Hazards**:
- Flying debris
- Noise
- Air line whip
- Pinch points

**Safety Measures**:
- Use safety clips on connections
- Wear hearing protection
- Never point at anyone
- Bleed air before disconnecting

### Grinders

**Wheel Safety**:
- Ring test new wheels
- Run new wheel 1 minute before use
- Never exceed max RPM
- Keep guards in place
- Use face shield

## Electrical Safety

### Shop Electrical Safety

**General Rules**:
- Know location of main disconnect
- Use GFCI protected outlets
- Don't overload circuits
- Keep electrical panels clear
- Report damaged cords immediately

**Extension Cords**:
- Use proper gauge for load
- Never daisy-chain
- Keep away from traffic
- Replace if damaged
- Unplug by pulling plug, not cord

### Vehicle Electrical

**Battery Safety**:
- Disconnect negative first
- Connect positive first (when reconnecting)
- Remove jewelry
- Wear eye protection
- Keep flames and sparks away
- Neutralize spills with baking soda

**Hybrid/Electric Vehicles**:
- Special training required
- High voltage hazards (300V+)
- Follow manufacturer procedures
- Use insulated tools
- Disable system before service

## Vehicle Safety

### Moving Vehicles

**In Shop**:
- Know where others are
- Move slowly
- Use spotter when needed
- Set parking brake when stopped
- Chock wheels on lifts/ramps

**Test Drives**:
- Valid driver's license
- Follow traffic laws
- Wear seatbelt
- Know vehicle before driving
- Return to shop safely

### Running Engines

**Hazards**:
- Carbon monoxide
- Moving parts
- Hot surfaces
- Noise

**Safety Measures**:
- Exhaust extraction system
- Keep hands away from fans/belts
- Stay alert
- Good ventilation

### Carbon Monoxide

**Symptoms of Exposure**:
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Nausea
- Confusion
- Loss of consciousness

**Prevention**:
- Use exhaust extraction
- Never run engine in closed space
- Install CO monitors
- Know warning signs

## Emergency Procedures

### First Aid

**First Aid Kit Requirements**:
- Bandages (various sizes)
- Sterile gauze
- Adhesive tape
- Antibiotic ointment
- Cold packs
- Eye wash
- Burn cream
- Scissors and tweezers
- Gloves
- First aid guide

### Emergency Response

**For Injuries**:
1. Ensure scene is safe
2. Call for help
3. Provide first aid if trained
4. Don't move seriously injured
5. Wait for emergency services

**For Fires**:
1. Activate alarm
2. Call 911
3. Evacuate if unable to control
4. Use extinguisher if trained
5. Meet at assembly point

**For Chemical Exposure**:
1. Remove from exposure
2. Flush with water (eyes: 15+ min)
3. Remove contaminated clothing
4. Seek medical attention
5. Bring SDS to hospital

### Emergency Contact Numbers

Post prominently:
- 911 (Emergency)
- Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222
- Shop owner/manager cell
- Local fire department (non-emergency)
- Nearest hospital

## OSHA Compliance

### Required Programs

**Hazard Communication (HazCom)**:
- Written program
- SDS for all chemicals
- Container labeling
- Employee training

**Personal Protective Equipment**:
- Hazard assessment
- PPE selection
- Training
- Maintenance

**Emergency Action Plan**:
- Evacuation procedures
- Emergency contacts
- Employee training
- Plan review

### Required Postings

- OSHA "Job Safety and Health" poster
- Emergency phone numbers
- Emergency exit routes
- Safety rules

### Required Training

- New employee orientation
- PPE use and maintenance
- Chemical safety/HazCom
- Fire extinguisher use
- Emergency procedures
- Job-specific hazards

### Record Keeping

**OSHA 300 Log**:
- Record work-related injuries/illnesses
- Post summary February 1 - April 30
- Maintain for 5 years

## Hazardous Materials

### Waste Disposal

**Used Oil**:
- Store in approved containers
- No mixing with other fluids
- Recycle through approved vendor
- Maintain records

**Coolant**:
- Recycle or dispose properly
- Never pour down drain
- Store separately from oil

**Batteries**:
- Core return to vendor
- Store upright
- Prevent leaking

**Solvents/Chemicals**:
- Dispose as hazardous waste
- Use licensed hauler
- Maintain manifest

**Tires**:
- Recycle through approved vendor
- May require tire fee
- Never burn

### Spill Prevention

**Requirements**:
- Secondary containment for bulk storage
- Spill kits available
- Trained personnel
- Response procedures
