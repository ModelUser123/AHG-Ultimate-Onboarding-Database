## Estimate Fundamentals

### Components of an Estimate

1. **Labor**: Time × Labor Rate = Labor Charge
2. **Parts**: Cost + Markup = Parts Charge
3. **Sublet**: Outside services (machine shop, towing, etc.)
4. **Shop Supplies**: Miscellaneous materials
5. **Hazardous Waste Fees**: Environmental disposal
6. **Tax**: Applicable sales tax

### Estimate Structure

```
Customer Information:
- Name, phone, email
- Vehicle: Year, Make, Model, VIN, Mileage

Concern/Request:
- Customer's description of problem or requested service

Inspection Findings:
- Technician's diagnosis and findings

Recommended Repairs:
- Itemized list with labor and parts
- Organized by priority (safety, needed, recommended)

Totals:
- Parts
- Labor
- Shop supplies
- Tax
- Total
```

## Labor Time Sources

### Labor Guide Sources

1. **Mitchell On-Demand**
2. **ALLDATA**
3. **Chilton**
4. **Motor**
5. **Manufacturer service information**

### Understanding Labor Times

**Published Time**:
- Industry standard time for a job
- Assumes normal conditions
- Based on average skilled technician

**Actual Time Considerations**:
- Rust/corrosion (add time)
- Previous improper repair (add time)
- Accessibility issues (may need additional operations)
- Model variations (verify correct time)

### Labor Rate Calculations

**Effective Labor Rate**:
```
Total Labor Revenue ÷ Total Billed Hours = Effective Rate
```

**Goal**: 90%+ of door rate

### Overlap/Concurrent Labor

**When to Apply**:
- Multiple repairs share some labor
- Example: Replace water pump AND timing belt

**Calculation**:
- Full time for primary job
- Reduced time for secondary job (remove duplicated steps)

## Parts Pricing

### Markup Guidelines

| Part Cost | Suggested Markup |
|-----------|------------------|
| $0-$5 | 100-150% |
| $5-$25 | 75-100% |
| $25-$75 | 60-75% |
| $75-$150 | 50-60% |
| $150-$300 | 40-50% |
| $300-$500 | 35-40% |
| $500+ | 25-35% |

### Parts Matrix Example

| Cost | Multiplier | Selling Price |
|------|------------|---------------|
| $10 | 2.5x | $25 |
| $50 | 1.8x | $90 |
| $100 | 1.6x | $160 |
| $250 | 1.45x | $362.50 |
| $500 | 1.35x | $675 |

### Parts Quality Levels

**OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer)**:
- Dealership/factory parts
- Highest cost
- Matches original specs exactly
- Best for warranty-concerned customers

**OES (Original Equipment Supplier)**:
- Same manufacturer, different box
- Lower cost than OEM
- Same quality

**Aftermarket (Premium)**:
- Third-party manufacturers
- Quality varies by brand
- Good balance of cost/quality
- Popular brands: Moog, Raybestos, Beck/Arnley

**Economy/Value**:
- Lowest cost
- Lower quality
- Shorter warranty
- Suitable for budget-conscious repairs

### Customer-Supplied Parts

**Considerations**:
- No parts warranty from shop
- No markup = reduced profit
- May increase labor rate
- Potential for wrong part
- Document policy clearly

**Suggested Policy**:
> "We can install customer-supplied parts with an additional $X labor premium. We cannot warranty parts we do not supply. If the part is incorrect or defective, additional diagnostic and labor charges may apply."

## Shop Supplies and Fees

### Shop Supplies

**Common Inclusions**:
- Rags and towels
- Cleaners and solvents
- Small hardware (clips, fasteners)
- Lubricants and grease
- Tape and sealer
- Absorbents

**Charging Methods**:
1. **Percentage of Labor**: 5-10% of labor charge
2. **Flat Fee per RO**: $5-$25
3. **Percentage of Parts**: 3-5% of parts
4. **Itemized**: Charge specific items used

### Environmental/Disposal Fees

**Common Charges**:
- Oil disposal: $2-$5
- Coolant disposal: $3-$8
- Tire disposal: $3-$5 per tire
- Battery core: May be credit
- Refrigerant: Included in service

**Note**: Check state regulations for requirements on disclosure.

## Estimate Presentation

### Priority Categories

**1. Safety (Must Do)**:
- Brakes below minimum
- Steering components with play
- Tires below legal tread
- Leaking brake lines
- Failed suspension joints

**2. Needed (Should Do)**:
- Components near failure
- Maintenance overdue
- Minor leaks
- Worn but not unsafe parts

**3. Recommended (Consider)**:
- Preventive maintenance
- Comfort items
- Maintenance coming due
- Upgrades

### Presenting Options

**Good-Better-Best**:

| Option | Description | Price |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Good** | Economy parts, minimum needed | $XXX |
| **Better** | Quality parts, recommended service | $XXX |
| **Best** | Premium parts, comprehensive service | $XXX |

### Verbal Presentation

**Structure**:
1. Thank them for waiting/patience
2. Explain what was found
3. Prioritize by importance
4. Present costs clearly
5. Ask for authorization

**Example**:
> "Thank you for your patience. I've completed the inspection on your vehicle and want to go over what we found. There are three items I'd like to discuss, organized by priority..."

## Profitability

### Key Metrics

**Gross Profit Targets**:
| Category | Target GP% |
|----------|------------|
| Labor | 70-80% |
| Parts | 40-50% |
| Sublet | 15-25% |
| Overall | 55-65% |

### Calculating Profitability

**Gross Profit**:
```
Revenue - Cost = Gross Profit
Gross Profit ÷ Revenue = GP%
```

**Example**:
- Parts sell: $200
- Parts cost: $120
- Gross Profit: $80
- GP%: 40%

### Improving Profitability

1. **Increase Labor Efficiency**: Reduce actual time vs. billed time
2. **Optimize Parts Matrix**: Review and adjust markups
3. **Reduce Comebacks**: Quality work = fewer warranty claims
4. **Increase Average RO**: Thorough inspections, recommend needed services
5. **Manage Discounts**: Limit and track discounting

### Discounting Guidelines

**When to Discount**:
- Multiple services at once
- Customer loyalty (carefully)
- Competitive situations
- Good-will gestures

**Discount Limits**:
- Set maximum discount % (e.g., 10%)
- Require manager approval for higher
- Never discount labor below cost
- Track discount reasons

## Estimate Checklist

Before presenting estimate:

- [ ] Verify all labor times are correct
- [ ] Confirm parts availability and pricing
- [ ] Include all related items (fluids, gaskets, hardware)
- [ ] Add shop supplies as appropriate
- [ ] Calculate tax correctly
- [ ] Organize by priority
- [ ] Proof-read for errors
- [ ] Prepare to explain each item
